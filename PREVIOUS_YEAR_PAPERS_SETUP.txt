PREVIOUS YEAR PAPERS SETUP

1. Replace your old index.html on Netlify with the index.html from this ZIP.
2. In Supabase -> SQL Editor, run the FULL updated setup.sql from this ZIP.
3. Do not put any service-role/secret key in index.html.
4. Login to the Admin Panel using your existing admin account.
5. Open Admin Panel -> Previous Year Paper.
6. Upload PDF + Year + Class/Exam + Subject + Title.
7. Students will see the papers under "Previous Year Papers".

IMPORTANT:
The previous-year-papers Storage bucket is PUBLIC so students can open free papers directly.
Your paid notes-pdfs bucket remains PRIVATE.
